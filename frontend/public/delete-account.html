Delete your Driftway account

Driftway, by iBookBinding Ltd · you don't need the Android app for this

On the web, now

  1. Open Driftway in your browser and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings (⚙ at the top), then Delete my account, and confirm.

Open Driftway

This works even if your account has been disabled. It needs no invitation. If you'd like a copy first, use Download my data on the same screen.

Or by email

If you can't sign in, write to stepan@ibookbinding.com from the email address you signed in with, asking for your Driftway account to be deleted. We'll confirm when it's done. We may ask a question to check the request is really yours.

What is deleted

  • Your Driftway account: your sign-in id, email address and display name.
  • Drives you saved to your account.
  • Feedback you sent while signed in.
  • Your place in meetups: your starting point, preferences and votes. Meetups you organised are deleted entirely.
  • Your sessions.

What is kept, and why

  • The record that an invitation was issued and used is kept, with the link to you removed, so the beta's invitations stay accountable.
  • Things saved on a device before you signed in are not linked to your account, so they cannot be found from it. Clear Driftway's site data on that device, or uninstall the app, to remove them. To have feedback you sent without an account removed, email us roughly when you sent it.
  • Your sign-in with Auth0 (our sign-in provider) is held by Auth0. Ask us by email and we'll delete it there too.
  • Our hosting provider's request logs (time, IP address and the address requested) expire on that provider's own schedule. They hold no search text, positions or meetup link secrets.

Deleting your account doesn't change anything held by apps you opened routes in, such as Google Maps.
