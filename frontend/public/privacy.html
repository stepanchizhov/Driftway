Driftway privacy policy

Version 1.2 · 10 October 2026 · applies to the Driftway web app and its Android app

In short. Driftway uses your location and what you type to make drives, walks and meeting places. It keeps as little as it can: routes and walks are made and then forgotten. It keeps your account and what you save until you delete them, and old meetups are removed on their own. There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold. You can download or delete your data from Settings at any time.

Who we are

Driftway is run by iBookBinding Ltd, which decides how this information is used. For anything in this policy, write to stepan@ibookbinding.com.

What Driftway does not keep

  • The drives and walks it makes for you, your start points, checkpoints and destinations, and your searches. They are used to answer you and then discarded. They are not written to our logs.
  • Your pram, carrier and weight details. They stay on your device and are sent only with a walk request, to judge the walk, and are not stored.
  • Your movements. Driftway uses your location only while you are using it, never in the background, and does not record trips.

Logs and limits

Our hosting provider records each request to Driftway's server: the time, your IP address and the address requested. Since version 0.8 these records never include search text, positions or the private parts of meetup links. To stop the service being overloaded, Driftway counts requests per IP address or per account in memory, for at most a day. These counts are not stored.

On your device

Driftway keeps your settings, your Home place, your most recent destination, your walk preferences and a random device id in your browser's storage. If you sign in, our sign-in provider's session is kept there too. Removing Driftway's site data, or uninstalling the app, removes them.

Why we use it

To provide the features you choose to use; to keep Driftway working, secure and within its providers' limits; and, from feedback, to make it better. Under UK data protection law these are our legitimate interests, and, for accounts, providing the service you signed up for.

Children

Driftway is for parents and other adults. It is not meant for children and does not knowingly collect information from them. A child's weight entered for a carrier stays on your device.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: Settings, then Download my data.
  • Delete your account and everything kept with it: Settings, then Delete my account, in the web app or the Android app. Both also work for an account that has been disabled, and neither needs an invitation. How to delete your account, including by email and without the app, and what is kept afterwards. Your sign-in with Auth0 is held by Auth0; ask us and we'll delete it there too.
  • Things saved on a device before you signed in are not linked to you. They stay on that device and are not exported or deleted with your account unless you move them onto it from Saved places.
  • You can also ask us to correct, restrict or stop using your information, or to delete things the app cannot reach. Write to stepan@ibookbinding.com.
  • If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or to your local data protection authority.
