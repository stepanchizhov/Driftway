driftway Help

Welcome

Drives and walks for little ones who sleep on the move.

Driftway plans a drive of the length you need, or one that gets you there by a set time, and brings you home. It finds walks for a pram or a carrier from where you are, and a fair place to meet another parent.

Open Driftway

Works in your phone’s browser. No account needed.

What’s inside

  • Still asleep

    Arrived, but the nap isn’t over? One tap finds a longer way there and opens it in Google Maps.

  • Plan a drive

    A round trip, or the long way somewhere. Say how long, or when you need to arrive, and choose from up to three drives.

  • Walk

    Walks for a pram, a carrier or just you, with steps, gates and surfaces checked against the map, and what isn’t known said.

  • Meet up

    A fair place to meet another parent. Neither of you sees where the other sets off from.

Your first five minutes

  1. Open Driftway and allow location when asked. It’s used only while you’re using the app, never in the background.

  2. Save Home: tap ★ Saved and add it. It stays on your phone.

  3. In Settings, say what wakes your little one: speed bumps, traffic lights, turning round, roadworks.

  4. Try a drive before you need one, then tap Feedback and tell us what you noticed.

  5. To keep it on your home screen: in Chrome, open the menu and choose Add to Home screen. On an iPhone, in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Want to know more?

Help in the app explains each part: how drives are chosen, what a drive’s card tells you, walks, and your privacy. It’s at the top of Settings.

Open Help Notes for testers

Safety first. Set up a drive before you set off, and don’t use your phone while driving. Car seats are designed for travel, not routine sleep: take breaks on longer drives. Driftway doesn’t replace advice from a health professional.
