Testing Driftway

For families in the closed beta · from version 0.8

Thank you for trying Driftway. It's still being built, and it changes every week. What you notice, especially what confuses you or doesn't work, is the most useful thing you can give us.

Before you start

  • Sign in with the invitation link you were sent. You can use the drives without signing in, but walks and saving to your account need it.
  • Allow location when asked. Driftway uses it only while you're using the app, never in the background.
  • To check which version you have, go to Settings and look at the top line, or tap What's new?.

What to try

  • Still asleep: the baby fell asleep in the car. One tap gives a drive that keeps going and brings you home.
  • Plan a drive: choose how long you want to drive, as a loop from home or on the way somewhere. Did the drive take about as long as it said? Did it ask you to turn around, or go down the same road twice?
  • Walk: choose Pram, Carrier or Just me and a time, then make walks from where you are. Try a checkpoint: choose it on the map, and set how close the walk must come to it in Walk preferences. Were the steps, gates and surfaces it warned about really there? Did it miss any?
  • Meet up: with another parent, find a place that is fair for you both. Each of you adds a starting point that the other doesn't see.

Telling us

  • Tap Feedback at the top of any screen. It tells us which screen you were on and which version you have, so you only need to say what happened.
  • After a drive, the "would you use it again?" question takes two taps. It's how the drives get better.
  • Please don't put your address in feedback. "The walk from the park gate" is plenty.
  • For anything else, write to stepan@ibookbinding.com.

Still rough, and known

  • Walks are made from map data, and nobody has walked most of them. Unknown surfaces are shown as unknown, not as fine.
  • Google Maps can't follow a walk exactly. Use the map in the app.
  • "Greener" and "Quieter" walks make little difference in Britain: the route provider has little data to choose with here.
  • If our usual route provider stops answering, drives still work, but their times don't include live traffic, and each drive says so.

Safety first. Set up the drive before you set off, and don't use your phone while driving. Car seats are designed for travel, not routine sleep: take breaks on longer drives. For slings and carriers, follow the TICKS guidance shown in the Walk tab. Driftway doesn't replace advice from a health professional.
